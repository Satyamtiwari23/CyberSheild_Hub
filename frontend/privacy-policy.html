Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Welcome to CyberShield Hub. This Privacy Policy explains how CyberShield Hub collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website and services.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account or use certain features, we may collect information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Password information
  • Information required to provide security scanning features
  • Basic technical information required for website operation

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Authenticate users
  • Provide password recovery functionality
  • Provide cybersecurity analysis and scanning features
  • Improve the functionality and security of the website
  • Respond to user requests and support inquiries

3. Password Security

User passwords are not stored in plain text. Passwords are securely hashed before being stored in our database.

4. Password Reset

When you request a password reset, CyberShield Hub generates a temporary reset token and sends a password reset link to the email address associated with your account.

Reset tokens are temporary and expire after a limited period of time.

5. Email Communication

We may use third-party email services to send account-related emails, including password reset messages.

These emails are used only for providing the requested account functionality.

6. Data Protection

We take reasonable technical measures to protect user information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee complete security of information.

7. Third-Party Services

CyberShield Hub may use third-party services for infrastructure, database storage, authentication, email delivery, hosting, or security-related functionality.

These services may process information as necessary to provide their respective services.

8. Cookies and Local Storage

CyberShield Hub may use browser storage technologies such as local storage to maintain user preferences or authentication-related information.

You can manage browser storage through your browser settings.

9. Data Retention

We retain account information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, comply with applicable requirements, or resolve disputes.

10. Children's Privacy

CyberShield Hub is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy
